How to Use

1
Start with Oil: Heat oil in a pan on medium flame before adding the masala for best flavor release and to prevent burning.
2
Right Amount: Use 1–2 teaspoons per serving as a starting point and adjust to your taste preference.
3
Add Mid-Cook: For curries, add after the onions are golden brown. For dry vegetables, add alongside the main ingredient.
4
Finish with Heat: A pinch sprinkled at the end gives an aromatic boost. Always cover after adding for 2 minutes.
5
Storage: Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Seal tightly after use to preserve freshness and aroma.
